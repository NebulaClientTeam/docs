Nebula is a Hypixel Skyblock client built around macros, automation, and quality-of-life features. Farm, mine, fight, and explore on autopilot. Nebula handles the grind with human-like precision while you do literally anything else.

## Get Started

Start with the [installation guide](/installation), then open the in-game GUI and look through the modules for the content you play.

In-game command to open gui are either:

```text
/n
/nebula
/nebulaclient
```

You can also bind a key in settings on the left.

## What's Inside

Nebula's features are grouped into modules, each with its own features and settings.

Full features list is available [here](https://nebulaclient.net/features).
